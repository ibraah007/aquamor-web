# Requesting a change to your website

You do not need to edit the website yourself. You send a request, and the person who maintains the site makes the change, checks it and publishes it.

This page explains what to send and what happens next. Keep it somewhere you can find it.

---

## How to send a request

Send a WhatsApp message or an email to:

| | |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp | `[to agree at handover]` |
| Email | `[to agree at handover]` |
| Urgent problems only (site down, wrong price showing) | `[to agree at handover]` |

You can send one message with several small changes; you do not need a separate request for each one.

---

## What to include

The clearer the request, the faster it is done. Please include:

1. **Where it is.** The page and section, for example "Contact section", "the order builder", "the price card" or "a photo in Our work".
2. **What it says now**, if you can copy it.
3. **What it should say**, written exactly as you want it to appear.
4. **Photos**, if the change involves one. Send the original file, not a screenshot of a screenshot.
5. **For a price change:** a photo of the new price list, a flyer, or a written message confirming the price.

### Copy-and-send template

```text
Change for the Aquamor website

Page / section:
What it says now:
What it should say:
Photo attached: yes / no
Price confirmed in writing: yes / no / not a price change
```

## Prices and business facts

The site only shows prices and facts that Aquamor has confirmed. If you ask for a price that has not been confirmed in writing, we will ask you to confirm it before it is published.

The current confirmed prices are refills at 5L Ksh 50, 10L Ksh 100 and 20L Ksh 200, and new 20L bottles at Ksh 1,400 (hard bottle with water) and Ksh 450 (regular bottle). Small bottled water, custom-branded bottles and dispenser cleaning are left as "quoted on request" until you give us the prices.

---

## Photos

- Send the original photo file if you can.
- Tell us what the photo shows, so the caption is accurate.
- If people can be recognised in the photo, confirm that they are happy for it to appear on the website.
- We will not use a photo that looks like it came from another business or from the internet.

---

## What happens next

1. We read the request and ask any questions if something is unclear.
2. We make the change on a copy of the site, check it on a phone and a computer, and in English and Swahili.
3. We publish it and tell you when it is live.
4. You check it and tell us if anything needs adjusting.

- The person who pays the yearly renewals is: `[to agree at handover]`.
- Keep the login details somewhere safe. Never send passwords, hosting logins or the site's admin key in a WhatsApp group or a public message. If you need to share one, send it to the maintainer directly.
- If you change hosting or the domain, tell the maintainer before the change, not after.

---

## Please do not

- Edit the website files yourself or ask someone else to "quickly change" them without telling the maintainer — it is easy to break the Swahili text, the price calculations or the photo gallery.
- Send customer names, phone numbers or addresses in a request. The website does not need them.
- Share the admin key or hosting password with anyone who is not maintaining the site.
