# Information we need from Aquamor

**Prepared 14 November 2026**

The website is almost ready. To finish it and put it live, we need you to confirm the items below. We only publish prices and facts that you have confirmed — that way a customer never sees a price or a claim that is out of date.

You can answer in one WhatsApp message, in a voice note, or by sending a photo of your price list. Please answer by the number next to each question so nothing is missed.

---

## 1. Prices

1. The **Ksh 500–800** you mentioned — is that **per bottle** or **per pack/carton**?
2. Does that range cover **small bottled water** or the **branded bottles** (bottles with your label)?
3. If it is small bottled water, what is the price for each size: **300ml, 500ml, 1L, 1.5L** (and 5L if you sell it)?
4. For **branded bottles**, what is the **minimum order**?
5. How much do you charge for **dispenser cleaning**?

## 2. Delivery

6. Which areas do you deliver to **free**?
7. Is there a **minimum order** to get free delivery?

## 3. Business details

8. What is the **official business name and tagline**? We have seen more than one version and want to use the correct one everywhere.
9. Do you want the **water purification steps** shown on the website? If yes, please list them.

## 4. Quick confirmations

10. Is your **Facebook page a business page** (not a personal profile)?
11. Which **bottle sizes do you actually sell**? The flyer shows 500ml, 5L, 10L and 20L, but your message also mentions 300ml, 1L and 1.5L.
12. The **regular 20L bottle at Ksh 450** — does that price include the water?
13. Do you **sell the water pumps**, or are they only for use with your dispensers?

## 5. Website address (domain)

14. What **website address** do you want? We suggest **`aquamor.co.ke`**. If it is taken, `aquamor.ke` or `aquamorwater.co.ke` are good alternatives.
15. Should the domain be **registered in the business name**, and **who will pay the yearly fee**? It costs about **Ksh 1,200–1,500 per year**; this is paid to the domain registrar, not to us.
16. Do you **already own a domain or use email on one**? Please tell us before we change anything, so your email does not stop working.

We recommend the domain is registered in **Aquamor's name**, with Aquamor paying the renewal. We will keep the technical settings (the "DNS") so the website stays online, but the domain itself belongs to the business.

## 6. Photos (optional)

We can add these if you have them. Please send the original photo files, not screenshots:

- the shop front on Musindi Road, taken from the street;
- graduation or corporate event photos;
- a close-up of a glass bottle with its label;
- original before/after photos for the cleaning service (the current ones are small crops from a flyer).

## 7. Online payments

Online payment is built but switched off. To switch it on, we need:

- an **approved Flutterwave business account** that can receive Kenyan shillings;
- the account's **secret key** and **webhook secret** (these are private — send them to the person who maintains the site directly, never in a group chat);
- confirmation of the **bank/M-Pesa account where payments should settle**, and **who handles refunds**.

Until these are supplied, customers order through WhatsApp as they do now. Nothing breaks.

## 8. Swahili check

The site is in English and Swahili. Before launch, one native Swahili speaker from your team should read it once and tell us if any wording sounds unnatural.
