## 2. What is still needed to go live

1. **Domain registration.** Register `aquamorwater.co.ke` under AQUAMOR PURIFIED DRINKING WATER. Aquamor should keep the registrar login, ownership and renewal control, and pay the yearly renewal. We only need the technical DNS access so the site stays online. Confirm that the domain has been registered and by whom.
2. **Hosting.** The preview is a demo: it cannot save orders and has no staff page. The live site needs a small hosting account that stays online all the time, at roughly Ksh 1,500–3,000 per month. Choose one:
   - **(a) Aquamor buys it** in the business name and sends us the login (recommended — Aquamor owns it), or
   - **(b) we buy it** and hand over the login at launch, or
   - **(c) you already have hosting** — send the login and we will check whether it is suitable.

   Do not buy an e-commerce or website-builder package; the basic plan is enough. Also tell us whether we should look after it every month (updates, backups, monitoring) or hand everything over after launch. Send any password through a password manager or read it on a call, never in a WhatsApp group.
3. **Swahili check.** The site copy has been reviewed internally. One native Swahili speaker from the team should read it once and tell us if any wording sounds unnatural.
4. **Optional photos.** Send original photo files, not screenshots, if you have them: the shop front from the street, graduation or corporate event photos, a close-up of a glass bottle with its label, and original cleaning before/after photos.
5. **Permissions.** The current build keeps the other customers' labels already in the gallery (Wave360 and the church labels) and the Paybill number on the website, as it is on your flyers. Tell us if you want either removed.

**Online payments are deferred to a later phase.** Customers order through WhatsApp as they do now. No Flutterwave account, API keys or webhook secret are needed for launch.
