A trusted drinking water partner in Ngara

Aquamor Purified Drinking Water was established in 2019 and is located along Musindi Road, Ngara, Nairobi. We are committed to quality purified drinking water and reliable customer service.

We serve households, businesses, churches and organizations with convenient and dependable water solutions. Whether you need water for your home, your business or a special event, Aquamor is here to serve you.

Questions people ask

What sizes do you sell?

We refill 5L, 10L and 20L containers, and we sell bottled drinking water in 300ml, 500ml, 1L and 1.5L bottles.

How much does a refill cost?

A 5L refill is Ksh 50, a 10L refill is Ksh 100 and a 20L refill is Ksh 200.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we offer free delivery. Tell us your area when you order and we will confirm.

Can I order bottles with my own label?

Yes. We make custom-branded bottled water for churches, weddings, graduations, corporate functions and other events. Try the label designer on this page, or message us on WhatsApp.

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday from 8:30am to 8:30pm, and Sunday from 12:00pm to 7:00pm.

How do I pay?

You can pay with Equity Paybill 247247, account number 395210. Confirm your total with us on WhatsApp first.

Do you clean water dispensers?

Yes. We clean and sanitize dispensers, including the tank and taps. Ask us for a quote on WhatsApp.

Where are you located?

Along Musindi Road, opposite Ngara Girls Secondary School, next to Ngara Heights building, Nairobi.
